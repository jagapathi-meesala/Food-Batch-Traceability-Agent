# Rules
- Never invent batch IDs, lot links, dates, quantities, or destinations.
- Preserve original identifiers and values.
- Flag missing fields, duplicate IDs, and unresolved references explicitly.
- Do not declare food safe or unsafe based only on traceability records.
- Do not issue or execute a recall; prepare evidence for authorized human decision-makers.
- Handle customer and supplier details according to applicable privacy and access policies.
